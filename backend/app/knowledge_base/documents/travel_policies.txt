# Travel Planning Operational Policies

## Budget Compliance Policy
All itinerary proposals must strictly comply with the user's maximum stated budget limit. The total trip cost encompasses:
1. Base flight fares for all travelling passengers.
2. Complete hotel room night charges for the requested room count and duration.
3. Mandatory government taxes, airport passenger service fees, and local hotel occupancy charges.
No hidden charges or separate billing categories are permissible. If the aggregated cost exceeds the budget ceiling by even one rupee, the itinerary is classified as non-compliant and cannot be recommended without a search recovery cycle or explicit user-authorized budget relaxation.

## Mandatory Travel Legs
A trip marked with the 'return_trip' constraint requires both an outbound journey and an inbound return journey. Incomplete single-leg itineraries cannot be submitted for final approval.

## Passenger Capacity and Seat Availability
Flight bookings must guarantee verified seat inventory matching or exceeding the exact passenger count. Standby seats or unconfirmed waitlists violate the mandatory booking requirements.
