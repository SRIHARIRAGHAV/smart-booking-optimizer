# Travel Cancellation and Refund Provisions

## Flight Cancellation Guidelines
- Cancellations requested at least 72 hours prior to scheduled departure are eligible for partial refund minus carrier processing surcharges.
- Non-refundable budget fares typically offer travel credit vouchers rather than cash refunds.
- Unverified flight records cannot guarantee cancellation terms.

## Accommodation Cancellation Rules
- Standard hotel bookings allow free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in date.
- Discounted promotional rates (budget-tier) may be strictly non-refundable upon reservation lock.
- Taxes and mandatory occupancy charges are refunded if the stay is cancelled within permitted grace periods.
