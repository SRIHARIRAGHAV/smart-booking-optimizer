# Preference Ranking and Trade-off Guidelines

## Priority Hierarchy
1. Mandatory hard constraints (Return flight leg, Room capacity, Passenger seat availability, Strict budget compliance) must ALWAYS be satisfied.
2. Soft preferences (Direct flights, City centre hotel location, High hotel rating, Morning departure) are evaluated only among itineraries that satisfy all mandatory constraints.
3. Scoring Weights:
   - Direct flight: +30 points.
   - City centre hotel: +25 points.
   - High rating (>=4.0): +10 to +20 points.
   - Morning flight departure: +15 points.
   - Budget savings bonus: Up to +10 points proportional to surplus under budget ceiling.
4. Preference Trade-off Rule:
   In cases where no single itinerary satisfies all soft preferences within budget, the optimizer should prioritize direct flight over suburban hotel trade-offs, unless explicitly directed otherwise.
